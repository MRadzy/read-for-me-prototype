Read For Me

A friendly reading companion for people who are blind or low vision. It pairs fast on-device AI transcription with 1-tap volunteer verification, clear privacy boundaries, and asynchronous multi-page reading.

Evaluation Walkthroughs

  • 1. Read a Medication Label Check the dosage directions right now and save them so you don't need to aim the camera again tomorrow. Steps: “Read Something” → “Medication label” → snap photo → “Save to My Readings”.
  • 2. Clarify Messy Handwriting The AI reads a handwritten reminder, but one line is unclear. Have a volunteer check and correct it. Steps: “Read Something” → “Handwriting” → “Check with a volunteer” → confirm.
  • 3. Listen to Saved Prescription Next morning, look up yesterday's saved medication directions and listen to them read aloud. Steps: From Home, open “My Readings” → tap your medication item → “Listen again”.
  • 4. Multi-Page Async Reading (“Read Later”) Send a 3-page letter to volunteers so they can transcribe it when free, without holding a live video call. Steps: “Read Something” → “Read Later (Several pages)” → add pages → submit.

Prototype Controls

“Spoken screen audio” vocalizes screens and text aloud using browser speech synthesis, demonstrating how a blind user navigates via voice.
